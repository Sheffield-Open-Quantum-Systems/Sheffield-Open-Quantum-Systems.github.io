About the Group

The Sheffield Open Quantum Systems (SOQS) group is based in the School of Mathematical and Physical Sciences at the University of Sheffield. Our research focuses on the theoretical description of quantum systems that interact with complex environments, bridging fundamental quantum theory with applications in quantum computing, quantum optics, and condensed matter physics.

We develop analytical and computational methods to understand decoherence, dissipation, and non-Markovian dynamics in realistic quantum systems. Our work spans master equations, tensor network approaches, and quantum thermodynamics.

We are part of the Sheffield Quantum Centre, a cross-departmental hub for quantum science and technology at the University of Sheffield.
