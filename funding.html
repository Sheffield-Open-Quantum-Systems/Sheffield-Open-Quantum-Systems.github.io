Funding Opportunities

We are always looking for talented and motivated researchers to join the group. Below are current and upcoming funding opportunities.

PhD Studentships

Open to UK and International Students

We regularly have funded PhD positions in open quantum systems theory. Projects cover non-Markovian dynamics, quantum optics, and quantum thermodynamics. Funding typically covers tuition fees and a tax-free stipend for 3.5 years. Please get in touch to discuss potential projects.

Postdoctoral Fellowships

Various Schemes Available

We welcome applications from researchers interested in applying for independent fellowships to join the group. We can support applications to EPSRC, Royal Society, Leverhulme Trust, and Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowships. Contact us well in advance of deadlines to discuss proposals.

Masters and Summer Projects

Available Annually

We offer MPhys/MSc project opportunities for students at the University of Sheffield, as well as summer research placements. These provide excellent experience in theoretical quantum physics research.
